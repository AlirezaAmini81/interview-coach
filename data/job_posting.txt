Position: Student employment (80 hours/month), OrKA project
Institute for Software Engineering and Theoretical Computer Science
Quality and Usability Lab, TU Berlin

Project goal: research-based development of an orchestrator agent that
enables scalable collaboration between text-based conversation agents,
sustainably expanded through simple integration of further technologically
diverse agents.

Tasks:
1. Research and analysis of current multi-agent and orchestration approaches (~25%)
2. Support in the implementation and testing of orchestration and agent functions (~30%)
3. Support in preparing and maintaining agent descriptions, interfaces and evaluation data (~10%)
4. Support in conducting and evaluating assessments, benchmarks and experiments on agent coordination and user interaction (~35%)

Must-have profile:
- Enrollment at a German university
- Excellent knowledge of conversational agents
- Very good knowledge of Large Language Models
- Excellent knowledge of multi-agent systems
- Good programming skills in Python
- Experience with data analysis, experiments, and prior experience with user studies
- Experience in scientific work
- Willingness to work primarily on TU Berlin campus
- Good German and/or English skills

Nice-to-have:
- Good knowledge of APIs, open-source frameworks, or common AI tools
- Experience in research or user evaluation
